Orders placed online #

This section applies when you order and pay online. It is in English only for now. The sections after it describe orders arranged by WhatsApp; where the two differ, this section applies to online orders.

Online checkout is offered only for the delivery countries listed in the country selector. For those countries the checkout page works out the delivery cost and shows it, with an estimated delivery time, before you pay. For any other country, order by WhatsApp as described below. Delivery costs and times per country: [TO BE CONFIRMED].

  • Made to order. We do not keep finished stock. Each item is produced after you pay, by a third-party production partner that also packs and ships it. Estimated times therefore include production.
  • More than one parcel. Items in one order can be made by different partners, so an order may arrive in more than one parcel, at different times. You pay one delivery price for the whole order.
  • Tracking. You get an e-mail when your order is confirmed and another for each parcel that ships, with tracking details where the carrier provides them. The private order link shows the status of every parcel.
  • Not every product goes everywhere. The checkout tells you if an item cannot be delivered to your country.
  • Duties and taxes. The checkout shows a notice where import duties or local taxes may be charged at the destination. Who pays them: [TO BE CONFIRMED].

Cancelling. Production usually starts soon after payment. Write to us as soon as possible, by WhatsApp on +34 622 20 99 35 or at [CONTACT EMAIL], with your order number (it looks like TCD-000000-XXXX). We can cancel only while no item of the order has gone into production. Cancellation conditions: [CANCELLATION POLICY].

Personalised items are made to your own specification, with the text you typed. How returns and the right to change your mind apply to them: [WITHDRAWAL RULES FOR PERSONALISED ITEMS].

Items with a standard design are also produced after you order. The return conditions for them: [RETURNS POLICY FOR NON-PERSONALISED ITEMS].

Returns and refunds. Always contact us before sending anything back: items come from different production partners, so we tell you where to send the item ([RETURN ADDRESS]). Refunds for online orders are made through the payment provider to the payment method you used. Your legal guarantee rights for faulty or damaged items are not affected.

How delivery is arranged #

There is no automatic shipping calculator on this website. When you send an order request through WhatsApp, we reply with the delivery cost and an estimated delivery time for your address. You see both before you pay anything.

Once your order is confirmed and paid, we prepare it and send you the tracking details, where the carrier provides them, in the same conversation.

Message us by WhatsApp on +34 622 20 99 35 as soon as you can. Include your order reference and a few photos of the item and the packaging.

How to start a return #

  1. Message us by WhatsApp on +34 622 20 99 35. Give your name, your order reference (it looks like TCD-000000-xxxx) and the item you want to return.
  2. Tell us the reason if you wish. For a damaged or faulty item, add photos.
  3. Wait for our reply. We confirm whether the return is accepted and give you the return address ([RETURN ADDRESS]) and instructions.
  4. Pack the item safely, unused and in its original packaging where possible, and send it as agreed. Keep the proof of postage.
  5. We confirm in the same conversation when the item has arrived.

Please do not send anything back before we have replied. A parcel that arrives without warning is hard to match to an order.
