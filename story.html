the idea

nice is only half of it.

Everybody has the side they show and the side they keep. We like both. So every piece here is easy to like at first look, and keeps one small thing for later: a detail, a note, a feature you find on the second day. The devil is just a wink. The secret is the point.

the founder

made by a nice guy. there's more to him.

a personal brand

The Cute Devil is the personal brand of Mohamed Khedr Mohamed Abdelmaged.

Valladolid, Spain · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Mohamed Khedr is an engineer and an educator. He builds smart devices, and he teaches.

Engineers like things that work. Teachers like things that make sense. The Cute Devil is where those two meet a third habit of his: he simply likes nice things.

So the brand has one rule. He makes the things he actually wants to own. If he would not wear it, wear it out or keep it on his desk, it does not get the name.
