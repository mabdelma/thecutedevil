Ordering #

How does ordering work?

Add what you like to the bag, open the checkout and type your name and your city and country. The site prepares a WhatsApp message with your items and an order reference.

WhatsApp opens with that message ready. You press send, and we reply in the same chat to confirm availability, the final price, delivery and payment. Your order is confirmed only in that conversation.

Why do you use WhatsApp?

Because the shop is new and we want to talk every order through with you. A chat lets us confirm stock, price and delivery with you directly before any money moves.

WhatsApp is operated by a third party under its own terms. If you would rather not use it, the contact page and the legal notice list the other ways to reach us.

Do I need an account?

No. The site has no accounts and no login. Your bag is saved in your own browser, on your own device.

Prices and payment #

Are the prices final?

Not yet. The prices on the site are samples that show the range we are aiming for. The final price, with any taxes and delivery cost, is the one we confirm to you in the WhatsApp conversation.

What does the “Sample” badge mean?

You will see this next to every price: Sample

It means the price is an example, not an offer. Totals in the bag are estimates based on those sample prices. Nothing is charged on the site.

How do I pay?

Not on this website. It takes no payment and never asks for card details. We agree the payment method with you in the chat when we confirm your order. Accepted methods: [PAYMENT METHODS].

Please never type card numbers into a chat message.

We plan to deliver in Spain, across the European Union and in the United Arab Emirates. Delivery times and costs are to be confirmed: [TO BE CONFIRMED]. For now we quote both for your address in the chat, before you pay.

How do I choose a size?

Products that come in sizes show the options on the product page, and you pick one before adding to the bag.

A full size guide is to be confirmed: [TO BE CONFIRMED]. Until then, ask us on WhatsApp for the measurements of any piece and we will help you choose.

Can I return something?

Start by messaging us on WhatsApp with your order reference, before sending anything back. We reply with the next steps.

Consumers in the European Union generally have a legal period to change their mind on distance purchases, with some exceptions. The exact conditions are being finalised and are set out on the shipping and returns page.

The site #

How do I change the colour theme?

Use the four round colour dots at the top of the page (inside the menu on phones) or in the footer. There are four looks: cream, red, purple and blue.

Your choice is saved in your browser so the site looks the same next time.

What does the site remember about me?

Two things, both kept on your device: your colour theme and your bag. No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no third-party scripts.

You can erase both on the cookies and storage page.
