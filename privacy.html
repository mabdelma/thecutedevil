The short version #

  • The website has no accounts and no login.
  • It sets no tracking or advertising cookies and loads no third-party scripts, analytics or fonts.
  • Where online checkout is available for your country, you can place and pay for an order on the site. We then store the order on our server: your contact details, delivery address, the items, the payment status and the shipment tracking.
  • Payment happens on a page hosted by a payment provider. Your card details never pass through this website and we never store them.
  • To make and deliver your order we pass the details needed to the payment provider and to the production and delivery partner that makes and ships each item.
  • We send you e-mails about your order (confirmation, shipping, cancellation or refund). We do not send marketing e-mails.
  • Where online checkout is not available, the checkout only composes a WhatsApp message that you choose to send. The contact form always works that way.
  • Your browser keeps a few small functional items on your own device: your colour theme, your language, your bag, your chosen delivery country and, after an online order, a reference to that order. Details are below.

Legal bases #

In general terms, we rely on the following grounds. The final wording will be confirmed after legal review.

  • Steps before and performance of a contract: handling your order or order request, payment, production, delivery, order e-mails, cancellations, returns and refunds.
  • Legal obligations: keeping invoices and tax records, and answering consumer claims.
  • Legitimate interests: replying to general questions, preventing misuse of the checkout, and keeping the website secure and working.
  • Consent: only where we ask for it, for example if we offer a newsletter in the future. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Online orders: payment, production and e-mails #

Payment. When you press the pay button, your browser is sent to a payment page hosted by a payment provider. We pass the provider what it needs to take the payment: the order number, the items and amounts, the currency and your e-mail address; for some providers and countries also your name and phone number. You type your card or other payment details only on the provider's page. The provider then tells our server whether the payment succeeded. The provider handles your payment data under its own privacy policy. Payment providers: [PAYMENT PROVIDERS].

Production and delivery. Items are made to order by third-party production partners, which also pack them and hand them to a carrier. Once your order is paid, we send the partner that makes each item what it needs: the recipient's name, delivery address, phone number and e-mail address, the items and any personalisation text. If your items are made by different partners, each one receives these details for its own parcel. The partner and the carrier send us back the production status and tracking details. Production and delivery partners: [PRODUCTION AND DELIVERY PARTNERS].

E-mails about your order. We send e-mails to the address you gave at checkout: an order confirmation, a message for each parcel that ships (with tracking where the carrier provides it), and a message if the order is cancelled or refunded. They are written in the language you used on the site where a translation exists. They are service messages about your order, not marketing, and are sent through [EMAIL PROVIDER].

Unpaid orders. If you start an order and do not pay, it closes by itself. For orders that were never paid, the contact and address details are erased from our server automatically 30 days after the order expired.

WhatsApp is a third party #

WhatsApp orders, and any message you send us there, travel through WhatsApp, which is operated by a third party under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not control how that service handles your data.

When you press a WhatsApp button on this website, your browser opens WhatsApp with a draft message. This website sends nothing to WhatsApp by itself: the message leaves your device only when you press send in WhatsApp.

If you prefer not to use WhatsApp, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] instead.

While you use the checkout, your browser also keeps what you typed in the form (name, e-mail, phone, address, note) and a one-time attempt number in session storage, so nothing is lost if you reload the page or come back from the payment page. Session storage is emptied when you close the tab, and these two items are removed once your order page shows the order as confirmed. A short-lived copy of the shop's settings (which countries are served) is kept the same way.

You can remove all of these at any time on the cookies and storage page.
