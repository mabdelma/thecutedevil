The short version #

  • This website sets no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies.
  • It loads no third-party scripts, analytics, pixels or embedded content.
  • It saves two functional items in your browser’s local storage: your colour theme and your bag.
  • Both stay on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored #

Local storage is a small space your browser keeps for each website. It works like a notepad on your device. Unlike a cookie, its content is not sent along with each page request.

KeyWhat it holdsWhyHow long
tcd:themeThe colour theme you picked (cream, red, purple or blue).So the site keeps your chosen look on your next visit.Until you clear it or pick another theme.
tcd:cartThe items in your bag: product, options and quantity. No prices, no personal details.So your bag is still there when you come back or open another page.Until you empty the bag or clear it.

These two items exist only to make the site work the way you expect, so the site does not show a consent banner for them. Whether that is the right approach will be confirmed in the legal review.

What is not stored #

  • No account, password or login session: the site has no accounts.
  • No payment details: the site takes no payment.
  • No name, phone number or address. What you type in the checkout or the contact form is used once to compose a WhatsApp message and is not saved by the site.
  • No identifier that could recognise you across visits or across other websites.

How to clear it #

This button removes both saved items from this browser on this device. Your bag will be emptied and the site will go back to its default cream look.

You can also do it from your browser:

  • open your browser’s settings, find the section for site data or browsing data, and remove the data stored for this website;
  • or browse in a private window, where saved items disappear when you close it.

If your browser blocks local storage, the site still works. It simply will not remember your theme or your bag between pages.

When you leave for WhatsApp #

Order and contact buttons open WhatsApp, which is operated by a third party. Once you are there, that service’s own cookie and privacy rules apply, not this notice.

If this changes #

If we ever add analytics, advertising tools, embedded media or online payment, this page will be updated first and, where consent is needed, you will be asked before anything is stored.
