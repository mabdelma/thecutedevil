How ordering works #

There are two ways to order. Which one you see depends on your delivery country: online checkout is offered only for the countries listed in the country selector on this website. For every other country, and whenever online checkout is switched off, ordering is by WhatsApp.

Ordering online

  1. You choose your delivery country and add products to the bag. The bag is saved only in your own browser.
  2. At checkout you enter your name, e-mail address, delivery address and, where asked, a phone number and a short note. The page shows the items, the delivery cost, any tax line and the total before you pay.
  3. You accept these terms and press the pay button. You are then taken to a payment page hosted by a payment provider, where you pay. In some countries cash on delivery may be offered instead; it appears at checkout only where it is available.
  4. When the payment provider tells us the payment succeeded, the order is confirmed. We send a confirmation to your e-mail address, and you get a private link to a page that shows the status of your order.

An order that is started but not paid is not a purchase. It closes by itself after a short time and nothing is charged. If a payment fails, you can try again from the checkout page.

We may be unable to accept or complete an order, for example if an item cannot be produced or cannot be delivered to your address. If that happens after you have paid, we contact you to agree what happens next, for example a refund for the affected items.

Ordering by WhatsApp

  1. You add products to the bag.
  2. You type your name, your city and country and, if you wish, a short note. The site turns your bag and these details into a ready-made WhatsApp message with an order reference.
  3. WhatsApp opens with that message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp yourself.
  4. We reply in the same conversation to confirm what is available, the final price, the delivery cost and time, and how to pay.

A WhatsApp message is a request, not a completed purchase. A WhatsApp order exists only once we have confirmed it in that conversation and you have accepted the confirmed price and delivery details. Until then, neither you nor we are bound. We may decline a request; if we decline, you owe nothing.

Made to order and personalised items #

We do not keep finished stock. Each item is produced after you order it, by a third-party production partner that also packs it and hands it to the carrier. Different items may be made by different partners, in different places.

Some products can be personalised, for example with a short text you type. A personalised item is made to your own specification. Please check your text carefully before you pay: it is produced as you entered it.

Personalisation text is checked before production. We may refuse text that is offensive, unlawful or infringes someone else's rights; in that case we contact you to agree a change or another solution.
