How ordering works #

Ordering happens in a WhatsApp conversation with us, not on the website.

  1. You add products to the bag. The bag is saved only in your own browser.
  2. At checkout you type your name, your city and country and, if you wish, a short note. The site turns your bag and these details into a ready-made WhatsApp message with an order reference.
  3. WhatsApp opens with that message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp yourself.
  4. We reply in the same conversation to confirm what is available, the final price, the delivery cost and time, and how to pay.

Your message is a request, not a completed purchase. An order exists only once we have confirmed it in that WhatsApp conversation and you have accepted the confirmed price and delivery details. Until then, neither you nor we are bound.

We may decline a request, for example if an item is not available or we cannot deliver to your address. If we decline, you owe nothing.
